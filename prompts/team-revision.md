Your team is not locked. The team you configured in the previous round is kept as
a starting point, and you may change any part of it this round: the number of
leeks, their levels, capital allocations, components, weapons, chips and the
script each one runs. A different composition can matter as much as better code,
so reconsider the team together with your scripts. Check a new composition with
preview_build and validate_team, then save it with configure_team before test
and submit; the latest configured team is the one that competes.
